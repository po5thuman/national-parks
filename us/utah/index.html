Utah National Parks Directory: "The Mighty 5"

Utah is home to five of the most visually stunning national parks in the United States. Navigate through each park hub below to unlock detailed hiking itineraries, accommodation analyses, and permit advice.

Zion National Park

Towering red sandstone cliffs, slot canyons, and famous trails like Angels Landing and The Narrows.

Explore Zion Hub →

Bryce Canyon

Home to the world's largest concentration of hoodoos, high-altitude amphitheatres, and dark sky stargazing.

Explore Bryce Hub →

Arches National Park

Over 2,000 documented natural stone arches, balanced rocks, and high desert geological formations.

Explore Arches Hub →

Canyonlands

Carved by the Colorado River into four unique districts offering rugged backcountry and panoramic mesas.

Explore Canyonlands Hub →

Capitol Reef

The Waterpocket Fold monocline featuring historic orchards, slot canyons, and golden rock domes.
